# Logging and Monitoring Notes

## What to add in a real enterprise setup

- Centralized logging with ELK Stack or Splunk
- Metrics with Prometheus and Grafana
- Health checks using Actuator
- Distributed tracing with Zipkin or OpenTelemetry
- Alerting for failed orders or payment issues

## Why it matters

These practices make the system easier to operate in production and help during interviews when explaining observability and reliability.
